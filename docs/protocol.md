# SLR protocol — latest snapshot

The review considers XAI explanations for speech emotion recognition using speech audio, speech-derived transcripts or both. Written-only emotion analysis and multimodal explanations in which speech evidence cannot be distinguished from non-speech modalities are excluded.

Four explanation dimensions are coded independently: D1 explanation–model relation, D2 temporal resolution, D3 directly explained target and D4 explanation method(s). Delivery, speech information and evaluation are evidential attributes; corpus, language, elicitation and architecture are contextual attributes.

The 2021–2026 search includes Scopus, IEEE Xplore, Web of Science, ACM DL, ACL Anthology and ISCA Archive, plus backward/forward citation searching in OpenAlex conducted on 7 November 2026. Title/abstract decisions lead to report-level full-text eligibility. Eligible reports are appraised using QA v2 (Q1–Q7, 0/0.5/1; sum ≥4/7 and Q1, Q4, Q5 >0). Each companion report remains in the report register but is merged in unique-study synthesis. The included synthesis comprises 61 studies represented in 62 reports.

Full-text decisions were not carried out independently in duplicate. The second-coder workbook is an uncompleted instrument, not evidence of inter-rater reliability.
