# Figure provenance

Figures 1 and 4–11 in `public/figures/paper/` are PNG crops of the prior 29-page manuscript snapshot `XAI_for_SER__A_Systematic_Review_and_Taxonomy-6.pdf`. Existing figures were preserved. These older images should be read as manuscript snapshots; current study-level values are in the versioned website data.

**Figure 2** was replaced on 17 November 2026 using the author-supplied `fig_prisma.pdf`. Both `fig02.pdf` (vector) and `fig02.png` (raster) are provided. The revised flow reports 86 full-text assessments, 66 QA appraisals, 62 passing reports and 61 distinct included studies.

**Figure 3** was replaced on 17 November 2026 using the author-supplied `fig_taxonomy(1).pdf`. Both `fig03.pdf` (unaltered vector PDF) and `fig03.png` (300 dpi raster) are provided. Its taxonomy counts refer to the 61 unique studies.

The dedicated Results and Coding agreement navigation pages were removed at the author's request. All 11 figures remain available in **Reproducibility → Research figures**, and the revised taxonomy graphic is also visible on the Overview page. Study-level interactive taxonomy remains on the Taxonomy page. Coding agreement datasets remain available as supplementary downloads, with no dedicated navigation page.

The visual palette is forest green, sage, muted brown, and cream, consistent with the manuscript.
