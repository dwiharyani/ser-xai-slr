# Figure provenance

The archived manuscript images (Figures 1 and 3–11) in `public/figures/paper/` were extracted from `XAI_for_SER__A_Systematic_Review_and_Taxonomy-6.pdf`, 29-page manuscript supplied on 7 November 2026. They are **raster crops of the submitted PDF** rather than regenerated charts. The crop coordinates are retained in `scripts/extract_manuscript_figures.py`.

**Figure 2 was replaced on 17 November 2026** with the user-supplied `fig_prisma.pdf` (preserved as `public/figures/paper/fig02.pdf` and rendered at 250 dpi as `fig02.png`). It depicts 86 full-text assessments, 66 quality-appraised reports and 62 included reports representing 61 unique studies. Figures 1 and 3–11 remain unchanged.

The interactive charts displayed on the Results and Taxonomy pages are different: their counts are computed on the client from `public/data/studies.json`, which contains the 61 unique included studies. Their purpose is interactive exploration, and exact figure visuals should be cited from the manuscript.

Color palette: forest green `#355f3b`, sage green `#5e7f63`, muted brown `#a68d73`, and cream `#f1ece2`, consistent with the manuscript's Figures 2–11.
